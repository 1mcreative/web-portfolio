About

The quest to make the ordinary extraordinary — and the impossible, possible.

I'm Neha Paul, an Experience Designer currently pursuing a Master of Design in Experience Design at NIFT Bangalore. Before that, I spent four years designing workspace and enterprise experiences for teams at Microsoft, ServiceNow, and Amazon as part of DSP Design Associates.

I believe in crafting experiences that are easy to use — the kind that make people wonder how they ever lived without them. A dash of creativity, a pinch of curiosity, and a whole lot of heart.
